# ExamNexus — demo narration script

**Download PDF:** [ExamNexus-Demo-Walkthrough-Script.pdf](../public/downloads/ExamNexus-Demo-Walkthrough-Script.pdf)

Formal screen-recording voiceover. Read only the **spoken** lines. `[ON SCREEN]` cues are for the operator and are not read aloud.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Target duration** | Approximately 4½ minutes |
| **Pace** | About 130–140 words per minute, measured and formal |
| **Spoken length** | 563 words |
| **Accounts** | An approved administrator account, then faculty, then student. Do not read emails or passwords. |
| **Browser** | Desktop or laptop. Lockdown cannot be demonstrated on a phone, tablet, or the mobile app. |

**Recording notes**

- Pause only where marked **(pause)**.
- Do not invent webcam proctoring. Integrity is browser lockdown and event logging.
- Optional tab-switch beat is marked. If omitted, continue from submission.

---

## 1. Homepage — about 25 seconds

`[ON SCREEN]` Open `/`. Hold the hero, then scroll About, How ExamNexus works, and features. Skip Team and Contact.

**Spoken**

This is ExamNexus, an intelligent assessment platform for campus use. Under the heading Assess smarter. Learn better., three roles share one system: students sit examinations, faculty create and review them, and administrators oversee accounts and academic structure. The workflow is join a class, create and schedule assessments, sit them under lockdown, then review results. We proceed to sign in.

---

## 2. Login — about 15 seconds

`[ON SCREEN]` Select Login (`/auth`). Show email, password, and Login.

**Spoken**

Access is by school email and password. Student and faculty registrations wait for administrator approval; each user is then sent to the matching dashboard. We sign in with an approved administrator account.

`[ON SCREEN]` Sign in.

---

## 3. Administrator — about 40 seconds

`[ON SCREEN]` Admin Dashboard. Indicate counts and charts, then open each tool as named.

**Spoken**

This is the administrator dashboard: pending approvals, password resets, subjects, assessments, and integrity events.

Under Manage accounts, the administrator reviews signups, roles, and access. Password resets issue a temporary password. Departments and courses form the catalog used at registration. Manage subjects creates subjects, assigns faculty, and sets class sections. Announcements may go to everyone, to teachers, or to students. Assessments is a read-only campus view. Exam logs collect integrity events. Export data produces reports and CSV files.

We continue as faculty.

`[ON SCREEN]` Log out. Sign in with an approved faculty account.

---

## 4. Faculty — about 40 seconds

`[ON SCREEN]` Faculty Dashboard. Show subjects and invite codes. Open a subject, then the assessment builder. Open Question Bank and Analytics; do not open Integrity yet.

**Spoken**

The faculty dashboard shows subjects, section invite codes, and enrolled students. Assessments are a quiz, an examination, or an activity. Questions may be written by hand, taken from a document, or generated by artificial intelligence. Formats include multiple choice, enumeration, identification, true or false, and essay. The instructor sets the schedule, time limit, passing rate, and whether scores are shown after submission. The question bank stores reusable items. Analytics, retakes, announcements, and exports complete the teaching tools.

Students will now sit an assessment. We return afterward for the integrity log.

`[ON SCREEN]` Log out. Sign in with an approved student account.

---

## 5. Student, warnings, and lockdown — about 1 minute 40 seconds

Read slightly more slowly. Observe **(pause)** on the lockdown rules.

`[ON SCREEN]` Student Dashboard, then My Assessments. Choose Take Assessment. Hold the Assessment lockdown dialog.

**Spoken**

The student dashboard shows subjects, upcoming work, and performance. Students join with an invite code. My Assessments lists work by subject; scores appear when the instructor releases them.

Assessments may be taken only on a computer or laptop—not on a phone, tablet, or the mobile app. The ready screen shows the title, item count, time limit, and instructor instructions. The student must read the lockdown rules.

**(pause)**

Once started, the student cannot leave until the work is submitted or the timer reaches zero. When time expires, answers are submitted automatically. Navigation and dashboard controls are disabled.

Copy, paste, right-click, and blocked shortcuts are minor alerts: logged for the teacher, not counted toward automatic submission.

Major violations—switching tabs, leaving fullscreen, Alt+Tab or another application covering the exam, or a second assessment tab—do count. The first two are warnings. The third submits the examination automatically, even if the student has not finished. Going offline does not auto-submit; answers remain on this device. Every incident is recorded and sent to the teacher. Begin assessment is consent to these rules.

`[ON SCREEN]` Begin assessment. Show fullscreen, integrity bar, and timer. Optional: switch tab once, then return and submit.

The lockdown environment is now in force: fullscreen, remaining alerts, a countdown timer, and answers saved on the device. Leaving the tab records the first of three major violations. When the student submits, answers cannot be changed.

`[ON SCREEN]` Submit, then log out.

---

## 6. Faculty integrity logs — about 30 seconds

`[ON SCREEN]` Sign in as faculty. Open the same assessment. Integrity tab. Open one student timeline.

**Spoken**

We return as faculty to the Integrity tab. Every event from the sitting is listed here. Students are ranked by major alerts; automatic submissions after three majors appear separately. Opening a student shows the timeline: time, major or minor, and the type—tab hidden, fullscreen exit, copy attempt, and related actions. Analytics still holds scores and time spent per question.

---

## 7. Close — about 10 seconds

`[ON SCREEN]` Remain on the timeline.

**Spoken**

ExamNexus joins administration, teaching, and a monitored examination environment in one platform—from the homepage to the integrity record of each sitting.

---

## Operator checklist

1. Homepage: hero, About, How it works, features.
2. Login as administrator.
3. Dashboard, accounts, password resets, catalog, subjects, announcements, assessments, exam logs, export.
4. Log out. Faculty: subject, builder, question bank, analytics.
5. Log out. Student: My Assessments, lockdown modal, begin, optional tab switch, submit.
6. Log out. Faculty: Integrity tab, one student timeline.
7. Closing line.
